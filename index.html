ADMISSIONS GUIDANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about admission eligibility, Bar Council approval, and campus facilities at Soarghaati Law College.

Is Soarghaati Law College approved by the Bar Council of India (BCI)? +

Yes. Soarghaati Law College is fully approved by the Bar Council of India (BCI), New Delhi, and affiliated to Soban Singh Jeena (SSJ) University, Almora. All graduates are eligible to enroll with State Bar Councils across India and practice as Advocates.

What is the eligibility for B.A. LL.B (5-Year Integrated)? +

Candidates who have passed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) in any stream (Arts, Science, Commerce) from a recognized Central or State Board with minimum qualifying marks (45% for General, 42% for OBC, 40% for SC/ST) are eligible for B.A. LL.B.

Who can apply for the 3-Year LL.B programme? +

Any graduate degree holder (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.Tech., BBA, etc.) from a recognized university with minimum 45% aggregate marks (40% for SC/ST) can directly apply for the 3-Year LL.B professional degree.

Are scholarships and financial aid available? +

Yes. Students can avail government scholarships via the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) and Uttarakhand State Social Welfare Department schemes for SC/ST/OBC and meritorious economic candidates.

What practical courtroom exposure is provided? +

Students participate in mandatory Appellate Moot Court trials on campus, undergo district court clerkships under senior advocates, visit Uttarakhand High Court, and work in our Pro Bono Community Legal Aid Clinic.
